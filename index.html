06 — Common questions

Frequently asked.

How long does a project take?

Most marketing sites and landing pages launch in 2–4 weeks. E-commerce platforms usually take 4–6 weeks. AI automation projects vary by scope but are often live within 1–2 weeks.

What does a project cost?

Most website projects fall between $350 and $799. Custom e-commerce and AI automation are scoped after a short discovery call, and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We are based in Chattogram, Bangladesh and work fully remotely with clients in the US, UK and beyond. Messages get a reply within 24 hours.

Who actually does the work?

Us — Saif and Sharafat Alamgir. Aerovise is a two-founder studio, so there are no account managers or junior subcontractors between you and the people building your project.

Can you redesign or rebuild my existing website?

Absolutely. We audit what you have, keep what converts, and rebuild the rest — faster, cleaner and mobile-first, with your content and SEO preserved.

What happens after launch?

You get a clean handoff, analytics and a support window to fix anything that comes up. Monthly maintenance for updates and improvements is available if you want ongoing help.

How do payments work?

Fiverr orders are protected by Fiverr’s escrow. For direct projects we agree on milestones and invoice up front, so you always know what is due and when.
